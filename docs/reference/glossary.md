# Glossary

```{glossary}
Bipartite coordination
  Coordination number counted only over neighbours of opposite formal
  charge, within calibrated pair cut-offs. See {ref}`theory-bonding`.

Deficit
  Bulk coordination minus actual coordination: the number of dangling
  bonds of a surface atom.

Detected planes
  Facet planes fitted tightly to the outermost atoms of the cut particle.
  They are used for passivation and reports.

Facet family
  The set of facets related by the crystal's point-group symmetry, written
  {hkl}. With proper rotations only, {111} and {$\bar1\bar1\bar1$} are
  separate families in zinc blende.

Mirror removal
  The default cation-removal policy of the {111} reconstruction: at most as
  many cations are removed on cation facets as vacancies were made under
  anion facets.

μ1, μ2, μ3
  Ligand sites bound to one, two or three cations: on-top, bridging and
  hollow sites.

Placeholder ligand
  A single pseudo-atom (e.g. Cl) with the charge of an X-type ligand,
  placed on an anion lattice site. It can later be exchanged for a real
  molecule.

Prepass
  Removal of under-coordinated surface atoms before the charge balance.

Role
  Classification of a surface atom as terrace (unique), edge or vertex.

Shell separator
  Distance halfway between the first and second cation–anion shells of zinc
  blende, $\tfrac12 b(1+\sqrt{11/3})$. A ligand closer than this to a
  cation counts as bonded to it.

Termination
  The atomic layer a polar facet exposes: cation-rich or anion-rich.

Virtual site
  The bulk lattice position of a missing neighbour of a surface atom.

X-, L-, Z-type ligand
  One-electron (anionic), two-electron donor (neutral) and two-electron
  acceptor (metal complex) ligands in the covalent bond classification
  {cite:p}`green1995`.
```
